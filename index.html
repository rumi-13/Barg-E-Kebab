About

Welcome to Barg-E-Kebab, where we bring the authentic flavors of Iranian cuisine to your table. Our menu is crafted with passion, featuring a selection of traditional Iranian feasts made from the finest ingredients and rich Persian spices. At Barg-E-Kebab, we believe in offering not just food, but an experience — one that captures the warmth, hospitality, and centuries-old culinary traditions of Iran. Whether you're craving succulent kebabs, aromatic koorma, or perfectly spiced shawarma, every dish is prepared with care to deliver an unforgettable taste. Join us for a true Persian dining experience and indulge in the essence of Iran, right here at Barg-E-Kebab.
